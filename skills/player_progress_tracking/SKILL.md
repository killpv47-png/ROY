## Procedure
1. Load the two most recent snapshots for the primary player.
2. Report trophy, exp, average level and card-count deltas with dates.
3. Highlight cards upgraded since the previous snapshot.

## Pitfalls
- Snapshots exist only for scans run through the bot.
